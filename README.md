1. **Light & Premium Luxury Design**:
   - Palette: Warm ivory base (`#FAF7F2`), champagne gold accents (`#D4AF37`, `#C5A059`), royal typography (`Cinzel`, `Cormorant Garamond`, `Plus Jakarta Sans`).

   - **Animation 2: Before & After Bridal Glam Slider** (Interactive draggable comparison slider showing raw elegance transformed into royal HD bridal perfection).

   - **Animation 4: Smooth Staggered Scroll-Reveal Animations** (Section headers, service lists, brand badges, and photo grids animate gracefully on scroll).
   - **Animation 5: Floating Consultation Drawer & Real-Time WhatsApp Booking Estimator** (Slide-out drawer that formats client requirements into a 1-click WhatsApp message).

3. **100% Real, Dynamic Admin Panel (`/admin`)**:
   - **Direct Cloudinary Image Upload**: Connected to cloud `gdkzinnv`. Uploads are permanently stored on Cloudinary CDN and immediately visible on the website.
   - **Gallery Manager**: Upload, categorize (Bridal, Reception, Haldi, Styling), caption, and delete photos.

   - **Services Manager**: Manage all 6 services (Bridal HD, Engagement, Haldi/Mehndi, Reception, Hair & Saree Draping, Destination Wedding), inclusions, and pricing tags.
   - **Artist Profile & Bio**: Update Arsh's biography, portrait image, experience years, and philosophy quote.
   - **Client Inquiries & Booking Tracker**: View leads submitted from the website form, toggle status (`New`, `Contacted`, `Confirmed`), 1-click WhatsApp client or Call client.
   - **Contact & Policies Manager**: Update phone (`7428701987`), WhatsApp, Instagram (`@arshkhan_makeupartist`), pincode (`110017`), Delhi NCR coverage, and policy terms (50% advance, 15-20 days notice).
   - **Real Password Management System**: Change admin master password anytime (Current Password &rarr; New Password &rarr; Confirm Password) with PBKDF2 cryptographic hashing.
